## Meeting #1 – Daily Standup, Blockers, Sprint Risk & Follow-ups

这次会议很适合学习**主持晨会**的英文。尤其是你在会议里反复使用的几类句型：**同步进度 → 问 blocker → 判断是否影响 Sprint → 确认完成时间 → 要求 follow up**。

---

# Topic 1. Starting the Meeting & Sharing Progress

## 📌 Meeting Background

Since several team members were still in another meeting, the team started first. The host shared her own progress before going through each team member's work. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 好，那我们先开始吧，因为其他人好像是在另外一个会议上面。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Alright, let's get started. It looks like the others are still in another meeting.

### Alternative Expressions

* Let's get started while we're waiting for the others.
* We can go ahead and start.
* The others can join us when they're done with their meeting.

---

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 那我先来同步一下我这边的进度。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Let me start by giving a quick update on my side.

### Alternative Expressions

* I'll start with a quick update from my side.
* Let me quickly walk you through my progress.
* I'll go first and share where things stand on my end.

### ⭐ 推荐直接背

> **Let me start with a quick update on my side.**

# Topic 2. Asking About Blockers

## 📌 Meeting Background

After reviewing a team member's tasks, the host noticed that there were quite a few items and explicitly checked whether anything was blocked or could affect delivery. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 我看你这边任务还挺多的，有阻塞的地方吗？或者有影响的地方吗？

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> It looks like you have quite a few tasks. Are there any blockers or anything that might impact your progress?

### Alternative Expressions

* Is anything blocking you right now?
* Are there any blockers we should be aware of?
* Is there anything that could affect the timeline?
* Do you see any risks at this point?

### ⭐ 晨会非常实用

> **Are there any blockers we should be aware of?**

> **Do you see any risks at this point?**

# Topic 3. Reporting a Blocker

## 📌 Meeting Background

One task required a database schema change from another team. However, the platform used for schema changes was having issues, so the task was temporarily blocked. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 他们的平台现在有点问题，还在处理，所以现在这边有一点阻塞。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Their platform is currently having some issues, so we're a little blocked on this task.

### More Professional

> We're currently blocked by an issue on their platform.

### Alternative Expressions

* We're waiting on the other team.
* This is currently blocked by an external dependency.
* We can't move forward until their platform issue is resolved.

---

## 🧠 Vocabulary

| Phrase               | 中文     |
| -------------------- | ------ |
| blocker              | 阻塞项    |
| external dependency  | 外部依赖   |
| be blocked by        | 被……阻塞  |
| move forward         | 继续推进   |
| resolve an issue     | 解决问题   |
| wait on another team | 等待其他团队 |

# Topic 4. Does This Affect the Sprint?

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 这个应该不影响本次 Sprint，对吧？会阻塞吗？

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> This shouldn't impact the current sprint, right? Is there any risk of it becoming a blocker? 

### Alternative Expressions

* Do you expect this to impact the sprint?
* Could this put the sprint at risk?
* Is this going to affect our delivery?
* Are we still on track?

### ⭐⭐⭐ 强烈建议背

> **Are we still on track?**

意思：

**我们还能按原计划完成吗？**

例如：

> Are we still on track to release tomorrow?

我们还能按计划明天发布吗？

# Topic 5. Waiting for Another Change Before Testing

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 等 Ivan 发到测试环境上才开始测试。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Testing will start once Ivan deploys the changes to the test environment. 

### Alternative Expressions

* We're waiting for the deployment before we can start testing.
* Testing is pending the deployment.
* We can't start testing until the changes are deployed.

### ⭐ 句型

> **We can't start X until Y is ready.**

例如：

> We can't start QA until the backend is ready.

> We can't release this until testing is complete.

# Topic 6. Asking About Completion Time

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 这个今天能结束不？

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Do you think we can get this done today?

### Alternative Expressions

* Can we finish this today?
* Do you expect this to be completed today?
* Are we on track to finish this today?

### ⭐ 推荐

比：

> Can you finish it today?

稍微柔和自然：

> **Do you think we can get this done today?**

# Topic 7. Sprint Deadline & Carry-over

## 📌 Meeting Background

The team noticed that a September 4 deadline coincided with the end of the current sprint. Development and test-case preparation could happen in this sprint, but actual testing might need to move into the next sprint. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 9 月 4 号就是这个 Sprint 的结束时间。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> September 4th is actually the end of this sprint.

---

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 我们正常写用例，可能要等到下个 Sprint 再测试。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> We can prepare the test cases in this sprint, but the actual testing may need to carry over into the next sprint.

### Alternative Expressions

* Testing may slip into the next sprint.
* We may need to push testing to the next sprint.
* The testing work will likely carry over.

### 🧠 区别

**carry over**
比较中性，只是“延续到下个 Sprint”。

**slip into the next sprint**
暗示**原计划没赶上，有延期**。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

# Topic 9. Following Up / 催一下

## 📌 Meeting Background

One task was waiting for confirmation from an overseas team. The host suggested following up with them that day. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 如果抽空的话，今天可以跟他们确认一下，或者催一催他们。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> If you have some time today, could you follow up with them and see if there's any update?

### Alternative Expressions

* Could you check in with them today?
* Could you follow up and see where things stand?
* Could you give them a quick nudge?
* Let's see if we can get an update from them today.

### ⭐ “催一下”不要直接翻译成 rush

工作场景最常用：

> **follow up with them**

稍微口语：

> **give them a quick nudge**

# Topic 10. Checking Whether Today's Deadline Is at Risk

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 我看这个截止时间是今天，这个有风险不？

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> I see this is due today. Is there any risk of missing the deadline? 

### Alternative Expressions

* This is due today. Are we still on track?
* Do you see any risk of this slipping?
* Is today's deadline still achievable?

### ⭐ 高频

**due today** = 今天到期

**miss the deadline** = 错过截止时间

**the deadline is still achievable** = 截止时间仍然可以达到

# Topic 11. Too Many Tasks — Focus on Today's Priorities

## 📌 Meeting Background

One team member had many temporary tasks, so instead of reviewing them one by one, the host asked what the main focus for the day would be. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 我看你这边临时任务很多，所以我们就不过一个一个了，就看你今天主要准备做什么。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> It looks like you have quite a few ad hoc tasks, so let's not go through them one by one. What are your main priorities for today?

### Alternative Expressions

* Let's focus on your priorities for today.
* What's your main focus today?
* What are the key things you're planning to work on today?

### 🧠 Vocabulary

**ad hoc task** = 临时出现的、非计划内任务

例如：

> I've had a few ad hoc requests come in today.

# Topic 12. Asking About Sprint Risk

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

### Alternative Expressions

* Is anything putting the sprint at risk?
* Do you anticipate any delivery risks?

---

## 💬 Original Chinese

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> I don't expect this to impact the current sprint.

# Topic 13. Closing the Standup

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 大家还有什么其他问题吗？

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Does anyone have anything else to bring up? 

### Alternative Expressions

* Anything else we need to discuss?
* Any other questions or concerns?
* Anything else before we wrap up?

---

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> **Anything else before we wrap up?**

# 📖 Grammar & Expression Notes

### 1. “有没有阻塞？”

最直接：

> **Any blockers?**

完整：

> **Are there any blockers we should be aware of?**

---

### 2. “会不会影响 Sprint？”

不要说：

> Will it influence the sprint?

推荐：

> **Will this impact the sprint?**

更自然：

> **Could this put the sprint at risk?**

---

### 3. “能不能按时完成？”

> **Are we still on track?**

加目标：

> **Are we still on track to finish this today?**

> **Are we still on track for Friday's release?**

---

### 4. “催一下”

推荐顺序：

> **follow up with them** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> **check in with them** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> **give them a nudge** ⭐⭐⭐

---

### 5. “临时任务”

> **ad hoc task**

而不是：

❌ temporary task

Temporary 更偏“暂时存在”；**ad hoc** 更符合“突然插进来的临时工作”。
