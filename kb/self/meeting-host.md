## Meeting #1 – Daily Standup, Blockers, Sprint Risk & Follow-ups

这次会议很适合学习**主持晨会**的英文。尤其是你在会议里反复使用的几类句型：**同步进度 → 问 blocker → 判断是否影响 Sprint → 确认完成时间 → 要求 follow up**。

---

# Topic 1. Starting the Meeting & Sharing Progress

## 📌 Meeting Background

Since several team members were still in another meeting, the team started first. The host shared her own progress before going through each team member's work. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 好，那我们先开始吧，因为其他人好像是在另外一个会议上面。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Alright, let's get started. It looks like the others are still in another meeting.

### Alternative Expressions

* Let's get started while we're waiting for the others.
* We can go ahead and start.
* The others can join us when they're done with their meeting.

---

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 那我先来同步一下我这边的进度。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> Let me start by giving a quick update on my side.

### Alternative Expressions

* I'll start with a quick update from my side.
* Let me quickly walk you through my progress.
* I'll go first and share where things stand on my end.

### ⭐ 推荐直接背

> **Let me start with a quick update on my side.**

# Topic 2. Asking About Blockers

## 📌 Meeting Background

After reviewing a team member's tasks, the host noticed that there were quite a few items and explicitly checked whether anything was blocked or could affect delivery. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

### Alternative Expressions

* Do you see any risks at this point?

### ⭐ 晨会非常实用

> **Are there any blockers we should be aware of?**

> **Do you see any risks at this point?**

# Topic 3. Reporting a Blocker

## 📌 Meeting Background

One task required a database schema change from another team. However, the platform used for schema changes was having issues, so the task was temporarily blocked. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

### More Professional

> We're currently blocked by an issue on their platform.

### Alternative Expressions

* We're waiting on the other team.
* This is currently blocked by an external dependency.
* We can't move forward until their platform issue is resolved.

# Topic 4. Does This Affect the Sprint?

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 这个应该不影响本次 Sprint，对吧？会阻塞吗？

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> This shouldn't impact the current sprint, right? Is there any risk of it becoming a blocker? 

### Alternative Expressions

* Do you expect this to impact the sprint?
* Could this put the sprint at risk?
* Is this going to affect our delivery?
* Are we still on track?

### ⭐⭐⭐ 强烈建议背

> **Are we still on track?**

意思：

**我们还能按原计划完成吗？**

例如：

> Are we still on track to release tomorrow?

我们还能按计划明天发布吗？

# Topic 7. Sprint Deadline & Carry-over

## 📌 Meeting Background

The team noticed that a September 4 deadline coincided with the end of the current sprint. Development and test-case preparation could happen in this sprint, but actual testing might need to move into the next sprint. 

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 9 月 4 号就是这个 Sprint 的结束时间。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> September 4th is actually the end of this sprint.

---

## 💬 Original Chinese

> 我们正常写用例，可能要等到下个 Sprint 再测试。

## 🇺🇸 Natural English

> We can prepare the test cases in this sprint, but the actual testing may need to carry over into the next sprint.

### Alternative Expressions

* Testing may slip into the next sprint.
* We may need to push testing to the next sprint.
* The testing work will likely carry over.

### 🧠 区别

**carry over**
比较中性，只是“延续到下个 Sprint”。

**slip into the next sprint**
暗示**原计划没赶上，有延期**。

# 📖 Grammar & Expression Notes

### 1. “有没有阻塞？”

最直接：

> **Any blockers?**

完整：

> **Are there any blockers we should be aware of?**

---

### 2. “会不会影响 Sprint？”

不要说：

> Will it influence the sprint?

推荐：

> **Will this impact the sprint?**

更自然：

> **Could this put the sprint at risk?**

---

### 3. “能不能按时完成？”

> **Are we still on track?**

加目标：

> **Are we still on track to finish this today?**

> **Are we still on track for Friday's release?**

---

### 4. “催一下”

推荐顺序：

> **follow up with them** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> **check in with them** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> **give them a nudge** ⭐⭐⭐

---

### 5. “临时任务”

> **ad hoc task**

而不是：

❌ temporary task

Temporary 更偏“暂时存在”；**ad hoc** 更符合“突然插进来的临时工作”。

Meeting #2 – Skill Release, Technical Investigation & Cross-team Coordination

这场会议特别适合学习三类英语：讨论方案是否值得做、排查 AI/服务问题、跨团队协调。另外还有不少晨会主持时可以直接复用的表达。

Topic 1. Sharing Today's Plan
📌 Meeting Background

The meeting started with a progress update. The previous day's PID work had been completed, and the goal for the day was to bring two development tasks to around 50% completion.

💬 Original Chinese

昨天这些 PID 已经做完了，今天计划把这两个需求的开发进展到 50% 左右。

🇺🇸 Natural English

I finished the PID work yesterday. Today, I'm planning to get these two development tasks to around 50% completion.

Alternative Expressions
My goal today is to get these two tasks about halfway done.
I'm aiming to make about 50% progress on these two items today.
I'll focus on these two development tasks today.
⭐ 推荐

I'm aiming to get this about halfway done today.

aim to = 计划 / 目标是……

Topic 7. AI Issues Can Be Hard to Reproduce
📌 Meeting Background

The team discussed why repeatedly calling an AI service was not a reliable way to reproduce the problem: AI outputs are inherently non-deterministic, so the same request may produce different results. Instead, investigation should focus on the actual input/output logs from the original failure.

💬 Original Chinese

我用同样的请求调了五六次，没有复现。

🇺🇸 Natural English

I tried the same request five or six times, but I couldn't reproduce the issue.

⭐ 高频

reproduce an issue

= 复现问题。

💬 Original Chinese

AI 本身就有这种随机性。

🇺🇸 Natural English

AI responses are inherently non-deterministic.

Alternative Expressions
AI output can vary between runs.
You won't necessarily get the same result every time.
The model's behavior isn't fully deterministic.
🧠 Vocabulary

deterministic = 确定性的

non-deterministic = 非确定性的

💬 Original Chinese

🇺🇸 Natural English

💬 Original Chinese

🇺🇸 Natural English

⭐ 非常好的排查句型

🇺🇸 Natural English

We should handle this on a case-by-case basis.

⭐⭐⭐ 值得背

Let's evaluate this on a case-by-case basis.

📖 Grammar & Expression Notes
1. “还在排查”

We're still investigating it.

更强调定位：

We're still trying to pinpoint the root cause.

2. “复现不了”

I couldn't reproduce the issue.

不要：

❌ I can't repeat the bug.

3. “拉某人进来”

loop someone in

例如：

Let's loop the AI team in.

4. “这是哪个团队负责的？”

Which team owns this?

负责团队：

the owning team

5. “两边规则不一致”

The rules aren't aligned.

There's a mismatch between the two services.

6. “先别过度设计”

如果数据量只有 300：

Let's keep it simple.

We don't need to over-engineer this.

over-engineer = 把简单问题设计得过度复杂。

7. “产品还没定”

We're waiting on a product decision.

或者：

The expected behavior hasn't been finalized yet.
