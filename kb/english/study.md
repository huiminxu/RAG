一、完整句子：中英文对照
1. 开场与客户反馈
I recently just got back from GroceryShop.
我最近刚参加完 GroceryShop 活动回来。
What stood out to me most was the feedback I received from my clients about their teams and how much they enjoyed working with us.
最让我印象深刻的是客户对我们团队的反馈，以及他们有多么喜欢与我们合作。
2. Red Bull Canada：Alyssa 和 Maryfer
I got the chance to meet with Red Bull Canada for the first time since they joined in August.
自从 Red Bull Canada 在八月份开始与我们合作以来，我第一次有机会和他们见面。
They just gushed about Alyssa and how happy and satisfied they are with her and her ownership of the Pacvue re-launch across five retailers.
他们对 Alyssa 赞不绝口，并表示非常满意她的工作，尤其认可她负责推动 Pacvue 在五个零售渠道重新上线时展现出的责任感。
They repeatedly talked about how responsive and organized Alyssa is.
他们反复提到 Alyssa 的响应非常及时，而且工作很有条理。
They also talked about how happy they are that they moved to Pacvue because of her.
他们还表示，因为 Alyssa，他们非常庆幸自己选择了 Pacvue。
原文是 moved Pacvue，从语义看应该是 moved to Pacvue 或 made the move to Pacvue。

Alyssa did all of this with only about five months of Pacvue experience under her belt after joining recently.
Alyssa 最近才加入公司，在只有大约五个月 Pacvue 工作经验的情况下，就完成了这一切。
A huge thank you to Maryfer Castro as well for all her diligent work setting up and maintaining the Canada account.
同时也非常感谢 Maryfer Castro，感谢她认真负责地完成加拿大账户的搭建和维护工作。
The future of Red Bull Canada is bright.
Red Bull Canada 项目的未来非常值得期待。
I can't wait to see how Alyssa grows the relationship.
我非常期待看到 Alyssa 如何进一步发展这段客户关系。
3. Red Bull US DoorDash：Veronica
Our Red Bull US DoorDash contact was not shy about how much she loved working with Veronica.
Red Bull 美国 DoorDash 业务的联系人毫不掩饰她有多喜欢和 Veronica 合作。
She talked about how competent, strategically minded and organized Veronica was.
她称赞 Veronica 能力很强、具有战略思维，而且工作很有条理。
These sentiments were echoed by a former DoorDash representative.
一位之前的 DoorDash 客户代表也表达了同样的看法。
He only worked with Veronica for a few months, but said he remembered how strong she was.
虽然他只和 Veronica 合作了几个月，但他说自己仍然记得 Veronica 的能力有多强。
He also remembered how quickly and effectively she took over on DoorDash.
他也记得 Veronica 当时多么快速而高效地接手了 DoorDash 业务。
The Perdue team continued to highlight Veronica's excellence as well.
Perdue 团队也继续肯定 Veronica 的出色表现。
4. Perdue：Jacob
When we met with the Perdue team, they were very clear about their excitement to grow on Amazon.
当我们与 Perdue 团队见面时，他们非常明确地表达了拓展 Amazon 业务的期待。
They were also very thankful for Jacob Phillips' attentiveness and effectiveness on the search and DSP launches.
他们也非常感谢 Jacob Phillips 在搜索广告和 DSP 项目上线过程中表现出的细致和高效。
These sentiments were backed up by Chris Perdue himself.
这些评价也得到了 Chris Perdue 本人的认可。
They are also very excited to have him take over DSP, as we finally got the contract signed.
由于我们终于签下了合同，他们也非常期待由 Jacob 接手 DSP 业务。
His body of work on DSS speaks for itself.
他在 DSS 方面积累的工作成果已经足以证明他的能力。
5. COE 报表工作：Daniela
Jacob and Veronica's good work on Perdue has been enabled by Daniela Toledo taking over our reporting for the COE team.
Jacob 和 Veronica 能够在 Perdue 项目上取得良好成果，也离不开 Daniela Toledo 接手 COE 团队的报表工作。
She has quickly and effectively slotted in.
她迅速而高效地融入了这个岗位。
She has taken feedback and consistently improved.
她认真接受反馈，并且一直在持续改进。
She is also starting to take on new, more complex projects despite being called upon for other LATAM clients.
尽管她还需要为其他拉美客户提供支持，但她已经开始承担新的、更复杂的项目。
I'm excited to see how Daniela continues to grow here at Pacvue!
我非常期待看到 Daniela 未来如何继续在 Pacvue 成长！
6. Red Bull 和 WellPet：Emil
A couple of our Red Bull contacts continued to heap praise on Emil Qiu for his consistently high level of detail and strong work.
Red Bull 的几位联系人继续高度赞扬 Emil Qiu，认可他始终细致且高质量的工作表现。
Internally, I have heard nothing but good things about the amazing job he continues to do on WellPet.
在公司内部，我听到的也全是对 Emil 在 WellPet 项目上出色表现的肯定。
Several team members have told me how much confidence that gives them for Ashley's maternity leave.
几位团队成员告诉我，Emil 的表现让他们对 Ashley 休产假期间的工作安排非常放心。
They know WellPet is in such good hands.
他们知道 WellPet 项目已经交给了非常可靠的人。
I continue to be immensely proud of Emil's growth as an individual contributor and a budding leader.
我一直为 Emil 作为独立贡献者的成长，以及他逐渐展现出来的领导能力感到无比自豪。
I feel so lucky that I get to be his manager.
我觉得自己能够成为他的经理非常幸运。
7. 感谢 Mark
Lastly, it goes without saying, but thank you, Mark Russo.
最后，虽然这可能不用多说，但还是要感谢 Mark Russo。
You are the boss of these teams, as well as my manager and one of my many mentors.
你不仅是这些团队的负责人，也是我的经理和众多导师之一。
None of this is possible without him.
没有他，这些成绩都不可能实现。
I am continuously grateful to work with all of you.
我一直都非常感激能够和你们所有人一起工作。

非常感谢你在我们的客户项目中给予的领导和支持。
I'm also extremely grateful to have you as my manager, Zach!
我也非常庆幸你是我的经理，Zach！
原文使用了省略结构：
Extremely grateful for your leadership and support on our accounts, and to have you as my manager, Zach!
非常感谢你对我们客户项目的领导和支持，也非常庆幸你是我的经理，Zach！

太喜欢这个团队了！

谢谢大家，你们都太棒了！
